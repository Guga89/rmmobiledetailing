Good to know

Common questions

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can get to the vehicle and the keys, you can go about your day.

Do you need my water or power?

We'll let you know when we confirm your booking. In most cases access to an outdoor tap and an outlet is helpful.

How long does it take?

Exterior details take about 1 to 1.5 hours, interior details 2 to 3 hours, and a full detail 3 to 4.5 hours depending on the vehicle.

How do I pay?

You pay when the job is done and you're happy with it. Ask us about accepted payment methods when you book.
